Anatomy of a platform

Inside Xcelsior.

A GPU marketplace has to treat every machine as a stranger. Four layers stand between a rented computer and the job it runs.

1

Version gating

Hosts with outdated drivers or runtimes never enter the pool.

2

Least-privilege container

The job gets only the rights it needs, and nothing of the host's.

3

Egress firewall

Outbound traffic is restricted, so a job cannot roam the network.

4

Sandbox kernel

gVisor or Kata puts a second wall between the job and the machine.

The path of one job

  1. Request

    A person or an AI agent asks for a GPU.

  2. Place

    A scheduler picks a vetted host on price and reputation.

  3. Isolate

    The job starts inside all four layers.

  4. Meter

    Usage is counted second by second.

  5. Settle

    The host is paid and the record is sealed.
